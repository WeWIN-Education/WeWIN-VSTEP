1. Đọc nhanh câu hỏi trước khi nghe để biết cần tìm thông tin gì.

The workshop starts at nine thirty and finishes at eleven. Please bring a notebook and your student card. You do not need to prepare a presentation. Đọc tiêu đề và câu đầu của mỗi đoạn để dự đoán chủ đề. Sau đó đọc câu hỏi, gạch từ khóa và tìm cách diễn đạt tương đương trong bài. Với câu hỏi “this/they/it refers to”, đọc cả câu trước và câu chứa từ quy chiếu.

## D. Ví dụ có phân tích

Đề hoặc tình huống:
Đọc đoạn văn và trả lời: What is the main purpose of the school garden project?

Đoạn đọc mẫu:
“Last year, Green Valley School turned an unused piece of land into a small garden. Students planted vegetables, measured the growth of different plants and recorded the amount of water they used. The project was not designed to produce food for the whole school. Instead, it helped students understand how science is connected to everyday life. Teachers also noticed that students became more willing to work in groups because the plants needed regular care. This experience encouraged the school to include more outdoor activities in its science classes.”

- Vì sao cách làm này phù hợp: câu “Instead, it helped students…” nói trực tiếp mục đích; các câu khác là chi tiết hỗ trợ.

Some of them later began using the library’s reading room and online catalogue. The change suggests that a library can remain useful even when people obtain much of their information from the internet.”

- Vì sao cách làm này phù hợp: “These activities attracted…” và “later began using…” cho thấy hoạt động mới kéo người dùng trở lại.

## D. Ví dụ có phân tích

Đề hoặc tình huống:
You cannot attend an English club meeting on Friday. Write an email to the club leader. In your email, explain why you cannot attend, suggest another time and ask what preparation is needed.

Đoạn bài viết mẫu:
“Dear Ms Lan,

I am writing to let you know that I cannot attend the English Club meeting this Friday because I have to take my younger brother to a medical appointment. I am sorry for the inconvenience.

Could we meet next Monday afternoon instead? I would also like to know whether I should prepare a short presentation for the next activity. If there are any materials that I need to read before the meeting, please send them to me.

Thank you for your understanding. I look forward to hearing from you.
